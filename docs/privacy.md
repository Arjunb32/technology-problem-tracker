# Beta privacy information

This information describes the planned public beta. The local demonstration uses sample records and demo accounts on the computer running it. Public hosting and real sign-in require the owner's provider configuration and release review.

## What is public

Problems, symptoms, product names, device and software setups, screenshots attached to public contributions, comments, solutions, test results, and public display names can be viewed by other people. Public contributions may be copied or shared by their readers. Never include passwords, access tokens, personal addresses, or confidential logs.

## Account and operational information

For real sign-in, Google, GitHub, or Microsoft authenticates you through Supabase Auth. The platform uses a provider-linked account identifier and profile information to identify contributions and enforce permissions. Supabase may hold your email address and authentication records; the community feed does not expose email addresses. The platform does not collect your provider password.

Your affected confirmations, votes, follows, test results, and contributions are stored to support the problem workflow. In-app notifications belong to your account. Content reports, account restrictions, and internal moderation notes are available to authorized moderators, rather than being published as part of the feed. The status history and a moderation explanation relevant to a public problem may appear publicly.

The browser stores sign-in session information and local unsent drafts so an interruption does not erase your work. People using a shared device should sign out and clear drafts or browser storage before handing it to someone else.

## Services and backups

Cloudflare hosts the website and API. Supabase provides the database, authentication, and image storage. These services may process network and operational information, such as IP addresses and request logs, under their own terms. The deployment region and available provider privacy information must be included in the final launch notice.

The owner maintains protected database and image backups outside the hosting project. Backups can contain public contributions and private account/moderation records. They are for recovery and must be kept with restricted access. This beta does not include advertising, payments, an added analytics tracking service, or AI processing of contributions.

## Corrections, removal, and retention

You can edit supported contributions and participation within the website. To request account removal, removal of accidentally shared private information, or a review of a moderation action, use the owner's published support contact. Hiding content from public view does not immediately remove it from recovery backups or internal moderation history.

Before the public beta opens, the owner must publish a real support contact, deployment region, retention periods for logs and backups, and a process for account removal and appeals. Those choices are launch requirements; this document does not claim that they have already been configured.
